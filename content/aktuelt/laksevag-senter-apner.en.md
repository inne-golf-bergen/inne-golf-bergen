Big news: on October 1 we open our third venue, at Laksevåg Senter.

As always it will be self-serve and open around the clock. Our own entrance opposite Baker Brun means you get in whenever it suits you — independent of the mall's opening hours.

**What you get in Laksevåg:**

- 2 × Simulator Lounge
- Free parking
- Own entrance opposite Baker Brun
- Open around the clock, 24/7

Booking opens closer to the date. Follow along here and on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/innegolfbergen) — we'll let you know the moment the calendar is live.
